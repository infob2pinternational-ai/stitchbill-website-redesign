A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before we
say hello.

What is StitchBill?

A billing app that brings customers back. StitchBill is being developed for clothing retailers in Kerala, with customer remarketing and repeat purchases as its central focus.

Can I use all these features today?

Customer re-engagement capabilities shown here are planned or in development. Contact us for current availability and demo options.

Is remarketing fully automatic?

Automatic remarketing has not been verified and is not promised. The journey on this page illustrates a proposed experience, not confirmed automation.

Will StitchBill guarantee repeat sales?

No. The aim is to support customer relationships and encourage return visits. Purchases depend on your customers, your store and many other factors.

How do I enquire or request a demo?

Message +91 88910 74715 on WhatsApp or email hello@stichbill.com. Tell us about your shop and ask about current demo availability.

What does it cost, and when can I start?

No pricing or launch date is confirmed on this website. Ask the team for current availability and written pricing before making a purchase decision.

Does it support GST billing, inventory or my existing software?

These capabilities, hardware requirements and integrations are not verified in the available source. Tell us what you use today so the team can confirm whether your requirements can be met.

Will it send WhatsApp messages to my customers?

This website sends no messages. Automated follow-up and WhatsApp integration are not confirmed product features. The WhatsApp buttons only open a conversation with the StitchBill team.

What should I share when I enquire?

Your store type, district and the main problem you want to solve are enough to begin. Please do not send customer lists, phone numbers belonging to customers, bills or payment details.

Do I need to share customer data for a demo?

No customer data is needed for an initial enquiry. Ask for a demonstration using fictional sample data and confirm the product’s privacy and data-handling arrangements before uploading any real records.
