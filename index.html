A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before we
say hello.

What is StitchBill?

A billing app that brings customers back. StitchBill is being developed for clothing retailers in Kerala, with customer remarketing and repeat purchases as its central focus.

Can I use all these features today?

Customer re-engagement capabilities shown here are planned or in development. Contact us for current availability and demo options.

Is remarketing fully automatic?

Automatic remarketing has not been verified and is not promised. The journey on this page illustrates a proposed experience, not confirmed automation.

Will StitchBill guarantee repeat sales?

No. The aim is to support customer relationships and encourage return visits. Purchases depend on your customers, your store and many other factors.
