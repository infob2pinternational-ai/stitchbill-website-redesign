Privacy Policy — draft

Draft for owner review · 29 October 2026. This describes this marketing preview, not a launched StitchBill application. The operator identity, enquiry practices and hosting details must be confirmed before publication.

What this website collects

There is no enquiry form, account registration or customer-data upload on this site. The illustrative cards contain fictional sample data. This preview does not include third-party analytics scripts, advertising trackers or cookies.

Enquiry-click measurement

A small script counts WhatsApp link activations by fixed page location (such as hero or footer) in this tab’s session storage. It does not record names, phone numbers, message content, IP addresses, identifiers or browsing history, and sends no analytics requests. Counts are not shared with StitchBill or combined across visitors. They normally clear when the tab session ends; browser session restoration may retain them. Storage blocking does not prevent enquiries.

View or clear this tab’s click counts. These are link activations, not confirmed conversations or sales.

When you contact us

WhatsApp and email links open external services. Information you choose to send is handled by those services and the enquiry recipient. For an initial enquiry, share only your store type, district and requirements. Do not send customer records or payment details.

Owner review required: confirm who receives enquiries, the purposes of use, authorised access, retention periods and the process for handling access or deletion requests. These practices have not been verified from the source.

Hosting and external services

A hosting provider may process technical request logs. The production provider, log retention and any additional analytics must be confirmed before publishing this policy. WhatsApp and your email provider have their own privacy practices.

Future product data

This notice does not authorise collection of retailer customer data. A product-specific privacy policy and appropriate data-handling arrangements are needed before real customer records are collected through a StitchBill application.

Questions

Contact hello@stichbill.com with privacy questions. Please avoid including sensitive information in the initial message.
