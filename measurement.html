Enquiry click diagnostics

Free, local diagnostics only. These counts belong to this tab session, not all website visitors. No counts are sent to a server. A click is not a completed enquiry.

Use the homepage WhatsApp links, then return here in the same tab to inspect their counts. Counts may be unavailable when browser storage is blocked.

Click diagnostics require JavaScript. Contact links on the homepage work without it.
